FORMULA

How do we turn counts into probabilities?

P(j | i) = X(i, j) ÷ X(i)

X(i, j) is how often word j appears near word i, and X(i) is the total for word i. Example: if "cat" appears near "purr" 30 times out of 200 co-occurrences, then P(purr | cat) = 30 ÷ 200 = 0.15.
